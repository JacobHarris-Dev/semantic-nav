**KAPPA THETA PI**  
**Constitution**  
**Phi Chapter**

**PREAMBLE**

We, the students of The University of Georgia, in order to define our purpose, functions, and progression, to attain our full potential, and continue the legacy of the Kappa Theta Pi Professional Fraternity, hereby establish this Constitution for the Phi Chapter of Kappa Theta Pi.

**ARTICLE I: Brand**

*Section 1: Name*

The name of this fraternity Chapter shall be “Phi Chapter of Kappa Theta Pi, Professional Technology Fraternity”. This document shall henceforth refer “Kappa Theta Pi, Professional Technology Fraternity” as “Kappa Theta Pi” or “KTP.”

*Section 2: Letters*

The letters of this fraternity shall be ΚΘΠ, which signifies a “love for technology.”

*Section 3: Purpose*

1\. Build an active community of students with a shared interest in technology.

2\. Sponsor events and activities in promotion of intellectual, technical, professional, and social development.

3\. Provide career guidance to members.

4\. Foster relationships among members, students, alumni, faculty members, the local community,  businesses, and other individuals and organizations whose relationship would be mutually beneficial.

5\. Provide service and philanthropy to the local community.

6\. Maintain a lifelong cooperation and friendship among the members of this fraternity.

7\. Kappa Theta Pi is committed to a policy of equal opportunity for all persons and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, marital status, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, disability, religion, height, weight, veteran status, or any other demographic category in its membership or activities. As well as follows The University’s  Non-Discrimination and Anti-Harassment Policy as follows:

1. Membership and all privileges, including voting and officer positions, must be extended to all students without regard to race, color, sex (including sexual harassment and pregnancy), sexual orientation, gender identity, ethnicity, or national origin, religion, age, genetic information, disability, or veteran status. 

2. Membership and all privileges, including voting and officer positions, must be extended to all students as stated in the University of Georgia Non-Descrimination and Anti-Harassment Policy. Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 protects people from discrimination based on race, color, or national origin in programs or activities that receive Federal financial assistance. Title IX states that: No person in the United States shall, on the basis of sex, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination under any education program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance. 

*Section 4: Affiliations*

Kappa Theta Pi \- Phi Chapter is affiliated with the national organization Kappa Theta Pi, though the Phi Chapter reserves all rights of governance and operations to the maximum extent possible for affiliation.

**ARTICLE II: Membership**

*Section 1: Terminology*

1\. The *Pledging* process shall be defined as the 6 weeks of the first semester seeking membership within the fraternity. A *Pledge* shall be defined as a potential member undergoing the Pledging process.

2\. The *Rush* process shall be defined as the process by which the fraternity is introduced to Pledge candidates and decides which candidates shall proceed into the Pledging process. A Pledge candidate undergoing the Rushing process shall be referred to as a *Rushee*.

3\. An *Active* shall be defined as a member of the fraternity who has successfully exited the Pledging process, is a current student at The University of Georgia, and is in otherwise good standing (see Article III) with the fraternity. Only *Active* members can vote on/decide organizational business, including elections, officer removal, quorum, and amendments.

4\. An *Inactive* shall be defined as a member of the fraternity who is not currently participating in fraternity requirements but will return to active status at the end of the semester.

5\. An *Alumnus* shall be defined as a former Active who has graduated from the University of Georgia and was in good standing with the fraternity in their last semester of enrollment.

*Section 2: Eligibility*

A Rushee who wishes to enter the Pledging process must reasonably expect to remain at the University for at least 3 semesters including their Pledging term. This section shall take effect one year after the establishment of the Phi Chapter.

*Section 3: Rush*

1\. At the beginning of the fall and spring semesters, a Membership Committee, chaired by the Director of Membership, shall arrange advertising, informational sessions, events, and applications for potential members.

2\. Rush shall begin with informational sessions that provide an overview of the fraternity to interested students. There shall be two to four sessions held at the beginning of each semester.

3\. After attending an informational session, all students interested in becoming members of Kappa Theta Pi must fill out an application to acquire clerical information and a summary of their interests. To promote academic achievement, only Rushees with a cumulative GPA of 3 or higher will be considered for membership.

4\. To become acquainted with potential members, three events shall be held for students interested in becoming active members. The Membership Committee shall administer these events. At each of the events, attendance and participation shall be recorded and considered for eligibility for attendance at future events. 

5\. The Executive Board shall conduct approximately 15 minute professional interviews with all qualifying potential members at the conclusion of the Rush process. No more than three Executive Board members shall be present at each interview. At the discretion of the Vice President of Membership, active members may participate in interviews.

6\. Within 24 hours of the last interview, a Selection Meeting of all eligible active members shall be held to determine the Pledge class. In order to vote at the selection meeting, attendance at two of the three Rush events is required for active members. The President shall preside over the meeting with the assistance of the Vice President of Membership. The Selection Meeting shall be conducted by the President and Vice President, and the members shall be presented by the Director of Membership. Via discussions and voting, bids shall be given out to potential members based on a holistic review of qualifications and the needs of the Phi Chapter.

7\. A Bid Night party shall be held within 72 hours of the selection meeting to welcome new students to the fraternity. At this point, the chosen rushees shall become Pledges and receive their Pledge token—a plastic ninja.

*Section 4: Pledging*

1\. As Pledges of Kappa Theta Pi, they shall be required to participate in the events and onboarding requirements decided by the Membership Committee designed to create bonds among other pledges and active members and foster commitment to KTP. Bids shall be rescinded upon failure to complete all of the requirements unless overridden by a 2/3  majority decision of the Executive Board.

2\. Big and Little pairings shall be decided halfway through the semester. One active member shall be paired with one Pledge. Secret gift offerings, informal interviews, or other requirements shall be decided by the Membership Committee. 

3\. At a Crossover event at the end of each rushing term (6 weeks), pledges who successfully complete the pledging process shall become active members.

4\. Successful pledges shall recite the following oath, administered by the Vice President of Internal Affairs or their designee: “I (name of pledge), do solemnly swear (or affirm) that I will faithfully support and defend the Constitution of the Phi Chapter of Kappa Theta Pi, to the best of my ability.”

*Section 5: Active Membership*

Upon recitation of the oath, all members agree to uphold the purpose and mission of Kappa Theta Pi.

Active members must meet all requirements, as stated in Article III.

*Section 6: Alumni*

1\. An alumni member shall be a person who has been successfully initiated into active membership to Kappa Theta Pi and graduated from The University of Georgia.

2\. At a Crossover event at the end of each rushing term, each graduating active member who meets the final semester lessened requirements shall become an honored alumni member. 

3\. Alumni members may attend any future event hosted by the fraternity.

4\. An annual newsletter shall be sent to alumni members, highlighting the Phi Chapter’s achievements and direction for the future.

5\. A Professional Big shall be a person who has been deemed helpful to KTP Phi Chapter by the President or 2/3 of the Executive Board and has graduated from The University of Georgia.

6\. Professional Bigs may be non-students/advisors but do not hold any power to vote in officer elections/removals, count towards quorum, or have a vote in constitutional amendments or organization business.

**ARTICLE III: Requirements**

*Section 1: Assessments*

1\. Active members shall maintain a minimum cumulative grade point average of 2.5.

2\. Active members shall not be a member of any other professional fraternity.

3\. Active members shall be able to name, draw, and explain the significance of the Letters.

4\. Active members shall be able to name and identify the date and location of the foundation, founding class, and current Executive Board.

*Section 2: Dues*

1\. Each semester, dues shall be determined by the Executive Board, and the funds collected shall be used for the reservation of rooms for Chapter meetings, funding social events, paying fees, or any other expenditure for the fraternity.

2\. The payments shall be collected by the Vice President of Finance and deposited into the fraternity’s official bank account. The Phi Chapter shall accept cash, checks, account transfers, and other payments at the discretion of the Executive Board. Cryptocurrencies and decentralized blockchain technologies shall not be used for any fraternity payment.

3\. Any additional fees may be assessed by the Executive Board at any time.

4\. If a member cannot afford dues, they must contact the Vice President of Finance in writing to determine an alternative plan, as seen fit by the Treasurer and approved by the Executive Board.

5\. Dues must be paid or an alternative plan must be created in the first month of every semester (January/September). Failure to meet this deadline will result in probation the following semester.

*Section 3: Community Service*

1\. Community service is encouraged, but not required. Active Members who participate in a philanthropy event  representing KTP may send in photo evidence to dismiss one of their first four absences. This can only occur once a semester. . 

*Section 4: Attendance*

1\. Attendance at Chapter meetings, professional development workshops, community service events, and social activities shall be logged.

2\. All active members are required to attend bi-weekly Chapter meetings and any event the Executive Board determines is mandatory.

3\. All active members will receive 4 free absences (excused and unexcused) per semester. If 5 absences are reached, the active member will be charged with a $50 fine and a talk with the judicial chair about their conduct. If 6 absences are reached, the active member is dropped. 

4\. Emergency absences are permitted through the emergency absence form. In order to have an absence waived, the active member must present some kind of evidence to prove an emergency (such as a severe illness, exams, car crash, etc.). Emergency absences are given at the discretion of the Judicial Chair/Board.

5.. If a member cannot attend an event, they must contact the appropriate member in writing.  
a. If the member is a pledge, they must contact the Brotherhood Chair (PM)  to receive an excused absence.  
b. If the member is active, they must contact the Judicial Chair/Board to receive an emergency excused absence.

*Section 6: Committees and Individual Tasks*

1\. Directors may choose to maintain a committee throughout the semester.   
a. If so, the committees will have meetings and aid the director in accomplishing   
	    tasks.  
2\. Directors who choose not to maintain a committee will be responsible for actively requesting help to accomplish tasks.  
	a. Tasks should be assigned via the task list, accessible through the drive or   
	    pinned post  
	b. Members should inform directors that they are working on a task, and they will   
	    be awarded appropriate points upon completion

*Section 7: Inactive* 

1\. Each member with active standing in the fraternity for one semester or more is entitled to one semester of Inactive Status at the members discretion.. Otherwise, additional semesters of inactivity will be determined on a case-by-case basis by the VP of Internal Affairs i.e.  study-away, internship, or personal reasons.

2\. To acquire Inactive Status, the requesting member must communicate with the VP of Internal Affairs prior to the Saturday before the KTP Bid Party.

3\. A member who must miss Chapter on each given week (whether for class or other reason) must use their Inactive Semester.

*Section 8: Probation* 

1\. Members who do not reach satisfactory points by the end of the semester  or do not pay dues by the Saturday before the KTP Bid Party. will be placed on probation status for the following semester and shall receive an probationary interview at the beginning of the following semester. Additionally, the member loses the right to a discretionary inactive semester.

2\. A probationary interview shall be a meeting between a member, and at least two Executive Board members. The exit interview is a formal way to review active status and future goals within KTP and give members an honorable way to drop out if they choose. If they choose to continue at KTP they must pay a reinstation fee set by the VP of Finance not to exceed 50% of per person dues during the probationary semester requirements.

3\. The Executive Board has the power to initiate the exit interview process with members at any time that it feels is appropriate.

4\. Probationary members shall not vote for the entirety of the probationary semester, except for the Selection Meeting notwithstanding other requirements for voting at the meeting elsewhere defined. 

5\. A member on probation is not eligible to attend any KTP events and social gatherings.

8\. Members placed on probation are subject to dismissal at the end of a semester, after having been on probation for one full semester.

*Section 9: Last Semester Alleviation*

1\. Members who maintain Active status are entitled to lower requirements in their final semester at the University:

1. Completion of the KTP Exit Survey, and  
2. Participation as a panelist in one Senior Panel (this may occur in either of their last two semesters at the University)  
3. Optional attendance at pledge meetings.

*Section 10: Prolonged Involvement* 

1\. All active members shall be expected to have three clean semesters in Kappa Theta Pi in order to crossover and obtain alumni status. The Executive Board maintains the right to override this requirement for extenuating circumstances.

2\. A clean semester is defined as any semester where an Active is not on probation (completes active requirements or chooses to take an inactive semester). The pledging semester does not count as a clean semester.

*Section 11: Confidentiality*  
1\. All Active Members, Pledges, Executive Board Members, and Cabinet Members shall maintain confidentiality regarding any internal, sensitive, or non-public information concerning the Phi Chapter of Kappa Theta Pi. This duty applies both during and after membership.  
2\. Confidential information includes, but is not limited to: 

1. Membership selection discussions and votes (e.g., Decision Night)  
2. Judicial or disciplinary matters, including strikes, PIPs \[Art. III §5\], and investigations by the VP of Internal Affairs or President  
3. Financial data, including bank records, budgets, or internal funding (members may still voice concerns to the VP of Finance, Exec Board, or Chapter)  
4. Internal deliberations held during Executive, Cabinet, or committee meetings  
5. Any meeting or message where confidentiality is explicitly invoked

3\. Officers may share information **only as necessary** to fulfill constitutional duties, report conduct violations, or meet legal obligations.  
4\. Unauthorized disclosure—verbal, written, digital, or otherwise—constitutes a violation subject to the Strike System or PIP \[Art. III §5\]. Severe or repeated violations may lead to Judicial Board action or removal from office or membership.

**ARTICLE IV: Bylaws**

*Section 1: Governance*

This Constitution shall be the supreme law of the Phi Chapter of  Kappa Theta Pi.

*Section 2: Chapter Meetings*

A bi-weekly Chapter meeting shall be held to discuss the current events and status of the fraternity. All active members and pledges—unless otherwise instructed—are required to attend all Chapter meetings. Prior written consent by a member of the Executive Board may excuse an absence from a meeting.

*Section 3: Social Gatherings*

A social gathering for members of this fraternity shall be held at least once per month. Funds from the fraternity's account shall be used to host these events.

*Section 4: Professional Development*

At least three professional development events shall occur per semester. These events may include but are not limited to resume-building workshops, professional photo shoots, internship panels, career counseling, and corporate visits.

*Section 5: Quorum*

A quorum must be present to conduct business. The quorum for any meeting shall count as ⅔ of all active members for that semester.

*Section 6: Voting*

Unless otherwise stated, motions require a simple majority of members present to succeed. A simple majority is 50% of members present plus 1 member.

**ARTICLE V: Leadership**

*Section 1: Purpose*

The Executive Board shall manage the fraternity and serve its members to the best of its ability.

*Section 2: Management*

1\. The Executive Board shall schedule Chapter meetings and events.

2\. Special meetings may be called by the President or their designated Vice President by giving 48 hours notice to all active members, unless circumstances deem otherwise.

3\. A weekly Executive Board meeting shall be called by the President.

4\. A weekly Executive Board office hour shall be held for any active member or pledge to attend. Any fraternity-related discussions may take place at this time, though no votes shall be taken except in an official meeting.

*Section 7: Executive Board Elections*

1\. Elections will be held annually in November. 

2\.  An eligible voter in an Executive Board election must maintain Active status on the date of the election.

3\. An eligible voter who is unable to attend the election may cast their vote by presenting a signed absentee ballot to the Executive Board before the start of the election.

4\. An eligible candidate for President, Vice President of External Affairs, Vice President of Internal Affairs, Vice President of Technical Development, Vice President of Professional Development, Vice President of Membership, Vice President of Marketing, must maintain Active status on the date of the election and have maintained Active status for at least one full semester.

5\. The application form should be sent out during the second week of November and remain open for the entire week.  
The following week, depending on the number of applicants for each role, there will be a debate for every position with multiple candidates. Incumbent members seeking to retain their current roles will also participate in the debate alongside new prospectives.

The Executive Board reserves the right to prepare and ask questions prior to the debate but is not required to do so. Actives within the fraternity are also encouraged to submit and ask their own questions during the debate. Questions do not have to be formally submitted prior to the debate, and can be asked on the day of the debate.

If a prospective has strikes on their record, the number of Personal Improvement Plans (PIPs) will be disclosed to the brotherhood audience but not the specific contents. Audience members may ask the prospective for additional context regarding their PIPs, although the prospective is not required to respond.

6\.Applicants must be able to commit to the role for an entire school year. Meaning those who would graduate in the Fall of the next school year, would not be eligible. Thus making the process much more streamlined and allowing for a universal shadow semester in the spring.

7\. It is the responsibility of the sitting Executive Board officers to serve as advisors for the incumbent officers throughout the semester after which the election takes place. The newly elected Executive Board officers shall take on their full responsibilities at the end of this semester when the election takes place (Fall). Full transference will occur within the first week of January.

8\. Only students who have paid their University of Georgia Student Activity Fee may serve as officers.

9\. During the last week of November or the first week of December, whichever includes our final chapter meeting, the new executive board will be announced.

*Section 9: Impeachment*

In the event that an Executive Board member, including the President, is believed to have failed to uphold the responsibilities of their office, engaged in conduct unbecoming of their position, or violated the fraternity’s standards or values, an impeachment process may be initiated.

An impeachment may be initiated by one-third (1/3) of the Chapter. Upon initiation, a spokesperson from the concerned party will give a presentation to the brotherhood and convene a meeting to allow the accused member an opportunity to respond.

Following the presentation, a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote from the active brotherhood is required to formally remove the individual from their position. If the impeached member is removed, a special election will be held to fill the vacancy in accordance with the established selection process.

*Section 10: Accountability*

A meeting of the Chapter, upon a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote, may overrule decisions of the Executive Board.

*Section 11: Judicial Board*

1\. The judicial board is to be composed of the Judicial Chair, and 4 additional elected members to serve as independent arbitrators over extraneous circumstances that may arise.  Each member, including the Chair, will receive one vote. 

2\. Elections occur at the beginning of each semester at the discretion of the Judicial Chair.

*Section 3: Origin*

Kappa Theta Pi Phi Chapter was established as a fraternity for students from the School of Computing and those pursuing technical majors at UGA, with a focus on fostering a broad interest in technology. The goal is to enhance community bonds among tech-oriented students and support their professional, personal, and social growth. The fraternity strives to be a nurturing network that promotes development and collaboration in a tech-centric setting, while also fostering lifelong friendships. 

**ARTICLE VII: Amendments**

*Section 1: Existence*

This fraternity may, from time to time, pass rules and regulations regarding the operation of daily business, as long as they are not contradictory with this Constitution and any current bylaws. 

*Section 2: Proposition*

Bylaws may be amended by a 2/3 majority vote. A change to a current bylaw shall be submitted in writing at an Executive Board meeting. Any active member may propose a change to the current bylaws.

*Section 3: Voting*

Voting on an amendment to bylaws shall be done at the next Chapter meeting with quorum in attendance.

*Section 4: Nondiscrimination*

Kappa Theta Pi is committed to a policy of equal opportunity for all persons and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, marital status, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, disability, religion, height, weight, veteran status, or other demographic categories in its membership or activities.

*Section 5: Routine Constitution Revision*

This Constitution is subject to yearly revision only once during the Fall semester in collaboration between Kappa Theta Pi Nationals, Phi Executive Board, and Phi Chapter. A revision is defined as “a change to the existing documentation,” and does not conflict with the ability to add amendments throughout the year. 

*Section 6: Abide to Nationals*

Phi Chapter must abide by University rules and amended National standards.

*Section 7: Misconduct*

Sexual Misconduct as defined by [University Policy](https://eoo.uga.edu/title_ix_sexual_misconduct/smpolicy/), and associated allegations of sexual misconduct will be reported directly to UGA’s [EOO](https://eoo.uga.edu/) (Equal Opportunity Office) if initiated by the reporting party. The result from the Equal Opportunity Office may lead to dismissal from the fraternity. The Vice President of Internal Affairs shall ensure compliance with this provision.

**ARTICLE VIII: Ratification**

This Constitution shall become effective immediately upon acceptance by a two-thirds majority vote of members present. The Constitution shall also be ratified annually at the last meeting of the year.
